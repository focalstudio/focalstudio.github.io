About

What we build and why

Focal Studio is an indie app studio focused on building apps that are genuinely useful — not growth-hacked, not ad-supported, not harvesting your data.

Our apps are small by design. Each one does one thing well. We care about privacy, honest UX, and shipping software that respects the person using it.

Everything is built with Capacitor and Ionic, keeping the codebase cross-platform and the experience native-feeling.
